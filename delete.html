🗑️ Delete your Ant Feast data

Ant Feast: Idle Colony · CanalGrid · com.canalgrid.antsidle

Ant Feast has no accounts of its own and we keep no data on our servers. Use the steps below to delete everything linked to you.

1. Game progress (on your device)

  1. Open the game → tap ⚙️ Settings.
  2. Tap Reset progress and confirm. All saved progress is deleted immediately.

Uninstalling the game also deletes it.

2. Google Play Games player ID & achievements

  1. Open the Google Play Games app (or Play Store → your profile → Play Games).
  2. Go to Settings → Delete Play Games account & data, find Ant Feast: Idle Colony under "Delete individual game data" and tap Delete.

This removes your player ID link and achievements for this game from Google's servers.

3. Advertising ID

Android Settings → Google → All services → Ads → Delete advertising ID (or Reset advertising ID). In the game you can also withdraw ad consent via Settings → Privacy options (where available).

4. Ask us

If you can't do the steps above, email YOUR-EMAIL@example.com with the subject "Ant Feast data deletion". We reply within 30 days. Since we hold no personal data on our servers, we will guide you through the steps above.

What is deleted / kept

Deleted: game progress, achievements, Play Games link to this game. Kept: Google Play purchase records (required by Google for billing and legal reasons; restorable via "Restore purchases").
