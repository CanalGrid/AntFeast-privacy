🗑️ Delete your Ant Feast data (iOS)

Ant Feast: Idle Colony · CanalGrid · com.canalgrid.antsidle

Ant Feast has no accounts of its own and we keep no data on our servers. Use the steps below to delete everything linked to you.

1. Game progress (on your device)

  1. Open the game → tap ⚙️ Settings.
  2. Tap Reset progress and confirm. All saved progress is deleted immediately.

Deleting the app also removes it (older iCloud backups may still hold a copy until they are replaced).

2. Advertising identifier (IDFA)

iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → turn off tracking for Ant Feast. In the game you can also withdraw ad consent via Settings → Privacy options (where available).

3. Ask us

If you need help, email YOUR-EMAIL@example.com with the subject "Ant Feast iOS data deletion". We reply within 30 days.

What is deleted / kept

Deleted: game progress and settings. Kept: App Store purchase records (held by Apple; restorable via "Restore purchases").
