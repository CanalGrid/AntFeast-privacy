🐜 Ant Feast: Idle Colony — Privacy Policy (iOS)

Last updated: October 2, 2026 · Developer: CanalGrid · Bundle ID: com.canalgrid.antsidle

This policy explains what information the iPhone and iPad version of Ant Feast: Idle Colony ("the game") handles, why, and how you can delete it. We do not run our own servers and we do not sell your personal information.

1. Data stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, upgrades, settings, achievements earned, and whether you bought "No Ads") is saved only on your device. We never receive it. If you use iCloud or computer backups, Apple may include it in your device backup. It is removed when you use Settings → Reset progress or delete the app.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob) and tracking

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), IP address, approximate location, and app interaction data to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud.

  • The game asks your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before the IDFA can be used. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the IDFA is not shared and you see non personalized ads.
  • You can change this any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • In the EEA, UK and other regions where it is required, you are asked for consent before ads load and can change it via Settings → Privacy options in the game.

See How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

3. Purchases

In app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. Use Settings → Restore purchases to restore "No Ads" on a new device.

4. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Data deletion

See Delete my data for how to remove your game data and advertising data.

6. Security and changes

Data sent by Google services is encrypted in transit. We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
