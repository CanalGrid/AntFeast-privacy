🐜 Ant Feast: Idle Colony — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026 · Developer: CanalGrid · App ID: com.canalgrid.antsidle

This policy explains what information Ant Feast: Idle Colony ("the game") handles, why, and how you can delete it. We do not run our own servers and we do not sell your personal information.

1. Data stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, upgrades, settings, achievements earned, and whether you bought "No Ads") is saved only on your device. We never receive it. It is removed when you use Settings → Reset progress or uninstall the game.

2. Google Play Games Services

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the game uses your Play Games player ID to unlock achievements. This data is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. We do not store your player ID ourselves.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, and app interaction data to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. In the EEA, UK and other regions where it is required, you are asked for consent before ads load and you can change it any time via Settings → Privacy options. See How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads / Privacy → Ads).

4. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details.

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Data deletion

See Delete my data for how to remove your game data, player ID and advertising data.

7. Security and changes

Data sent by Google services is encrypted in transit. We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
